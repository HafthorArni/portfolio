02M.Sc. thesis · 2025-2026

Motion into
power.

A sealed internal generator for a self-powered monitoring buoy, developed from electromagnetic modelling through prototype construction and repeatable bench testing. I also built the rope-driven simulator used to reproduce vertical wave motion.

Architecture
Three-coil carriage on a segmented magnetic rod
Control
50 Hz PID + feedforward, soft limits, watchdog
Result
8.8 W predicted inside a strict 1.8 m envelope

Profile

I like
making stuff :P

I’m Hafþór, a mechatronics engineer who likes owning the whole path from an idea to a working machine.

My professional work has centered on electronics and embedded hardware that must survive outside the lab. My M.Sc. thesis added mechanism design, real-time control, instrumentation and physics modelling to that mix. The home builds are where the habit started and where I still test unfamiliar ideas quickly.

I’m based in Reykjavík and relocating to Amsterdam. I’m most useful in teams that design, build, test and improve real hardware rather than stopping at the drawing.
